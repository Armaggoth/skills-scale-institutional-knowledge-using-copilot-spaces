# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA and Test Engineers ensure product quality, validate acceptance criteria, and identify defects before release. They collaborate with developers and product teams to maintain quality standards and provide confidence in production readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Perform manual testing for features, regressions, and edge cases
- Automate regression tests and contribute to test coverage
- Identify, document, and triage defects
- Participate in Definition of Done and quality reviews
- Validate fixes and sign-off on feature acceptance

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce production defects and customer-impacting issues
- Maintain efficient test coverage and automation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA estimation discussions
- Bug reports and defect triage sessions
- Quality metrics in weekly standups
- Release readiness sign-off and smoke test coordination

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test design, reproduce and validate fixes, clarify acceptance criteria
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria and feature scope, sign-off on quality gates
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics and release readiness status for timeline decisions

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, design guidance, and ensure solutions align with system architecture and technical standards. They mentor developers and mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture for new features
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and scalability concerns
- Establish and maintain coding standards and best practices
- Mentor developers and conduct code reviews for complex changes
- Support estimation and identify technical complexity early
- Ensure solutions integrate well with existing systems

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and well-architected solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Mentor team and share technical knowledge

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk assessments in planning
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Technical deep-dives and documentation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Mentor through code reviews, provide design guidance, support estimation
- **Project Managers**: Flag technical risks, dependencies, and complexity factors for timeline/scope decisions
- **QA / Test Engineers**: Collaborate on testability and integration points, define quality criteria

---

## Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove blockers, and help teams continuously improve their delivery practices. They coach the team on Agile principles and ceremonies.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team identify and resolve blockers
- Coach teams on Agile practices and continuous improvement
- Protect team focus and manage interruptions
- Track sprint metrics and identify process improvements
- Support cross-team collaboration and dependency management

### Goals
- Improve team velocity and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Enable self-organizing, high-performing teams

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and ceremony facilitation
- Retrospective notes and action item tracking
- Sprint metrics and process coaching
- Cross-team synchronization and blocker escalation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Partner on sprint planning, risk escalation, and dependency management; Scrum Master focuses on process, PM focuses on overall project timeline
- **Developers**: Support team health, remove process blockers, facilitate collaboration
- **All Roles**: Facilitate ceremonies and continuous improvement discussions

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business requirements and technical implementation. They ensure that features deliver business value and that acceptance criteria accurately capture the intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify business requirements from stakeholders
- Develop detailed acceptance criteria and user stories
- Validate that solutions deliver intended business outcomes
- Document requirements and business rules
- Collaborate with product and technical teams on feasibility and trade-offs
- Support user acceptance testing and stakeholder sign-off

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and testable
- Reduce scope creep and rework due to unclear requirements
- Deliver features that meet business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering and clarification sessions
- Acceptance criteria refinement in planning
- UAT coordination and stakeholder validation
- Requirements documentation and change management

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Translate business objectives into detailed requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements, answer questions during implementation, support technical feasibility discussions
- **QA / Test Engineers**: Ensure test cases align with acceptance criteria, support user acceptance testing
- **Stakeholders**: Gather requirements and validate solutions meet business needs

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are individuals or groups invested in project outcomes, including business leaders, customer representatives, and partner teams. They provide direction, approval, and feedback to ensure projects remain aligned with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide input on priorities and trade-offs
- Approve major decisions and milestones
- Provide feedback and validate outcomes
- Communicate project status and impact to their organizations

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value aligned with strategy
- Maintain transparency and alignment across the organization
- Reduce surprises and rework due to misalignment

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Milestone approvals and decision gates
- Risk and escalation notifications
- Release announcements and impact briefings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Provide business context, priorities, and success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Review status updates, approve major decisions and timeline changes
- **Business Analysts**: Provide business requirements and validate solutions
- **All Roles**: Receive progress updates and provide strategic direction
